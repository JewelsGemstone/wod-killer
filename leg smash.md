# 🧨 Leg Smasher Workout – “THE DESCENT”

**Goal:** Maximum leg fatigue (quads, glutes, hamstrings)  
**Style:** High-volume conditioning + sustained power  
**Equipment:** Echo Bike, kettlebell  
**Duration:** ~30–40 minutes  
**Warning:** Expect soreness and stairs to feel personal tomorrow

---

## 1️⃣ Warm-Up (6–8 minutes)

- Echo Bike — 3 min building pace  
- Walking lunges — 20 steps  
- Air squats — 25 reps  
- Hip openers / leg swings — 30 sec each  

---

## 2️⃣ Main Event: Echo Bike + Kettlebell Chipper

**For Time**

### 💀 Buy-In
- Echo Bike — **40 calories**

### 🔥 Leg Chipper
- 60 Goblet squats  
- 50 Alternating reverse lunges (25/leg)  
- 40 Kettlebell swings  
- 30 Jump squats  
  - *Scale:* Air squats if needed  
- 20 Bulgarian split squats (10/leg)

### 💀 Buy-Out
- Echo Bike — **40 calories**

> Break reps as needed. Expect quad failure during lunges.

---

## 3️⃣ Quad & Glute Obliterator (EMOM 10)

**Every Minute on the Minute — 10 minutes**

### Minute 1
- Echo Bike — 15 calories

### Minute 2
- 12 Front-rack kettlebell squats  
- 12 Kettlebell Romanian deadlifts

> Goal: Finish each minute with little to no rest.

---

## 4️⃣ “Can’t Walk Tomorrow” Finisher

**4 Rounds**

- Wall sit — 45 seconds  
- Tempo air squats (4-sec descent) — 15 reps  
- Rest — 30 seconds

---

## 5️⃣ Cooldown (Mandatory)

- Couch stretch — 2 min/side  
- Hamstring stretch — 1 min/side  
- Deep breathing until heart rate normalizes

---

## 🔧 Scaling Options

- Echo Bike: 30 calories instead of 40  
- Goblet squats: 40 reps instead of 60  
- Remove jump squats if impact is an issue  

---

## 🧠 Notes

- Echo Bike pre-fatigues legs before squatting volume  
- High-rep squats + lunges target quad endurance  
- EMOM forces repeated power output under fatigue  
- Wall sits finish remaining muscular capacity
