# Sustainable High-Energy, High-Protein Longevity Diet

This diet is designed to support:
- Daily energy and mental clarity
- Strength training and recovery
- Gut health and low inflammation
- Hormonal balance and long-term health
- Simplicity and sustainability over decades

It is **not a short-term diet** — it is a repeatable lifestyle template.

---

## Daily Meal Structure

### Breakfast
- ½ cup oats (dry)
- Transparent Labs protein powder (1 scoop)
- ¼ cup Steve’s Granola

**Why it works:**
- Slow-digesting carbohydrates for morning energy
- High-quality protein for muscle preservation
- Fiber for gut health and satiety

---

### Lunch
- 2 eggs  
- Rice *or* sweet potato  
- Lean protein: chicken, turkey, beef, or fish  
- Kimchi (fermented vegetables)

**Why it works:**
- Complete amino acids
- Glycogen replenishment for training
- Probiotics for digestion and inflammation control

---

### Dinner
- Rice *or* sweet potato (no eggs)
- Lean protein: chicken, turkey, beef, or fish
- Kimchi

**Optional additions:**
- Avocado oil (especially with sweet potatoes)
- Olive oil drizzle on protein

**Why it works:**
- Supports recovery and sleep
- Stable blood sugar overnight
- Micronutrient and electrolyte support

---

### Snacks (as needed)
- Bone broth
- Apple with honey *or* peanut butter
- Walnuts and/or macadamia nuts

**Why it works:**
- Collagen and joint support
- Natural carbohydrates without crashes
- Healthy fats for hormones, brain, and satiety

---

## Key Food Swaps & Cycling

### Rice
- Best for heavy training days
- Faster digestion and glycogen replenishment

### Sweet Potatoes + Avocado Oil
- Best for recovery or evening meals
- Higher fiber, potassium, and fat for stable energy

---

## Supplement Support
- Vitamin C (split doses)
- Bone broth (collagen + electrolytes)
- Optional: magnesium, omega-3s, vitamin D (seasonal)

---

## Estimated Nutrition Profile (Flexible)

- **Calories:** ~1,800–2,100 (scaled by training & stress)
- **Protein:** ~120–135 g/day
- **Carbohydrates:** ~160–190 g/day
- **Fats:** ~65–85 g/day

This range supports:
- Lean mass preservation
- Training performance
- Hormonal and metabolic health

---

## Health & Longevity Benefits

- Stable energy (no blood sugar crashes)
- Strong gut microbiome (kimchi + fiber)
- Anti-inflammatory fat profile
- Muscle and bone preservation
- Scales easily with age and activity level

## Guiding Principle

> Eat enough to fuel strength, recovery, and life —  
> adjust portions with demand, not guilt.

This diet prioritizes **consistency, quality, and adaptability** over restriction.
