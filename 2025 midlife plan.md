---

## ⚖️ 1. Safe Intermittent Fasting Framework

| Approach | Fasting Window | Eating Window | Frequency | Notes |
|-----------|----------------|----------------|------------|--------|
| **Daily 16:8** | 16 hr fast | 8 hr eat (e.g., 11 AM–7 PM) | 4–6 days/week | Supports fat loss, thyroid-neutral |
| **Weekly 24-hr (Dinner-to-Dinner)** | 24 hr | – | 1×/week (Sun) | Accelerates fat burn; keep activity light |

**Avoid:**  
- 36-hour or multi-day fasts (can lower T3 and raise reverse T3)  
- Fasting >2×/week  

---

## 🥗 2. Nutrition Guidelines (Support Thyroid & Muscle)

**Calories:** ~1,450–1,500 kcal/day (deficit), up to 1,650–1,700 kcal on heavy lift days  
**Macros:**  
- **Protein:** 135–150 g/day (≈1.0–1.1 g/lb BW)  
- **Carbs:** 100–130 g/day (for T4→T3 conversion)  
- **Fats:** Balance remainder from avocado, olive oil, nuts, etc.

**Micronutrients & Supportive Foods**
| Nutrient | Target / Source | Purpose |
|-----------|-----------------|----------|
| Selenium | 100 µg (1–2 Brazil nuts) | T4→T3 conversion |
| Zinc | 8–11 mg (meat, seeds) | Hormone synthesis |
| Iodine | 150 µg (iodized salt/seaweed) | Thyroid hormone substrate |
| Iron | Ferritin >70 ng/mL | Cellular oxygenation |
| Vitamin D | 1000–2000 IU | Metabolic & mood support |
| Creatine | 5 g/day | Strength, cognition, lean mass |

## 💧 4. Fasting-Day Protocol (Sunday Example)

**Fluids:**  
- 2–3 L water  
- Electrolytes (salt + LMNT/Nuun)  
- Black coffee, green or herbal tea allowed  

**Activity:**  
- Gentle walk or yoga only  

**Breaking the Fast (Sun 7 PM):**
1. Warm bone broth or herbal tea  
2. Main meal → salmon or chicken + quinoa + vegetables + olive oil  
3. Add 1–2 Brazil nuts (selenium)  
4. Finish with hydration and light stretching  

**Monday Breakfast ("Thyroid Boost"):**  
Eggs + oats + banana + chia or flax + coffee/tea
