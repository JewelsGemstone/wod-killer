# 🏋️ Back Squat Strength + Echo Bike & Kettlebell Conditioning

**Focus:** Lower-body strength + high-power conditioning  
**Style:** One heavy lift + complementary conditioning  
**Equipment:** Barbell, Echo Bike, Kettlebell

---

## 1️⃣ Warm-Up (10–12 minutes)

**General**
- Echo Bike — 5 min easy (nasal breathing)

**Mobility & Activation**
- World’s Greatest Stretch — 3 reps/side  
- Banded lateral walks — 12 reps/side  
- Glute bridges — 15 reps  

**Squat Prep**
- Goblet squats (light) — 10 reps  
- Pause air squats (3-sec bottom) — 5 reps  

---

## 2️⃣ Heavy Lift: Back Squat (Strength)

### Option A – Classic Strength
- Back Squat  
  - 5 sets × 3 reps @ 82–88% 1RM  
  - Rest: 2.5–3 min  
  - **Cue:** Last rep of each set includes a 1-second pause at the bottom

### Option B – Volume Strength
- Back Squat  
  - 4 sets × 5 reps @ 75–80% 1RM  
  - Rest: 2–3 min  

**Optional Accessory (Light)**
- Tempo Goblet Squat (3-1-1) — 2 sets × 8 reps  

---

## 3️⃣ Conditioning: Echo Bike + Kettlebell EMOM

**Duration:** 12–15 minutes  
**Structure:** Alternate minutes (EMOM)

### Minute 1 – Echo Bike
- 12–15 calories  
- Effort: ~85% (hard but repeatable)

### Minute 2 – Kettlebell Complex
- 8 kettlebell swings  
- 6 goblet squats  
- 4 clean & press (2/side)

> Use a moderate-heavy kettlebell. Aim to stay unbroken.

**Total Rounds:** 6–7

---

## 4️⃣ Optional Finisher (Optional)

**Echo Bike Sprint**
- 30 sec all-out  
- 90 sec easy pedal  
- 3 rounds

---

## 5️⃣ Cooldown (5–8 minutes)

- Couch stretch — 1 min/side  
- Pigeon or figure-4 stretch  
- Breathing: 4-sec inhale / 6-sec exhale

---

## 🧠 Notes
- Single heavy lift to preserve CNS recovery  
- Echo Bike provides high power with low joint stress  
- Kettlebell work reinforces posterior chain and core  
- Scale calories or KB load to maintain movement quality
