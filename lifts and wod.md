# Heavy Lift + Hard Follow-Up (Today)

Choose **Option A (Deadlift)** or **Option B (Bench)** based on how you feel after warm-up.  
Goal: **One heavy lift + one hard workout** (no extra heavy lifts).

---

## Option A — Heavy Deadlift + Engine Destruction

### 1) Heavy Deadlift (Strength)
**Goal:** low volume, high quality reps

**Work Sets**
- **5 × 3 @ ~85% 1RM**
- Rest **2–3 min**
- Cues: **tight lats**, **brace hard**, **patient off the floor**, **fast lockout**

**Optional Back-Off (only if you feel great)**
- **1 × 8 @ ~70%** (smooth, controlled)

**Quick Warm-Up Idea**
- 3–5 min easy cardio
- Hip hinge patterning (RDLs, empty bar good mornings, or light KB deadlifts)
- 2–4 ramp-up sets to working weight

---

### 2) Hard Follow-Up — 18-Minute AMRAP
**AMRAP 18 minutes** (as many rounds as possible)

- 12 **Heavy KB Swings** (Russian or American)
- 10 **Box Jumps** *(or step-ups if needed)*
- 8 **Burpees**
- 12/10 **Echo Bike calories** (12 cals men / 10 cals women)

**Intent**
- Explosive hips + breathing under fatigue
- Push pace, but **never sloppy**

**Scaling**
- Swings: reduce weight or reps to **10**
- Jumps: switch to **step-ups**
- Burpees: reduce to **6**
- Bike: cut to **8/6 calories**

---

## Option B — Heavy Bench Press + Upper Body Grinder

### 1) Heavy Bench Press (Strength)
**Goal:** heavy doubles with pristine setup

**Work Sets**
- **6 × 2 @ ~87–90% 1RM**
- Rest **2–3 min**
- Cues: **tight upper back**, **controlled descent**, **leg drive**, **bar path consistent**

**Optional Technique Work**
- **1–2 paused singles @ ~80%** (only if bar speed is good)

**Quick Warm-Up Idea**
- Shoulder prep (band pull-aparts, scap push-ups)
- 2–4 ramp-up sets to working weight

---

### 2) Hard Follow-Up — For Time (5 Rounds)
**5 rounds for time**

- 15 **Push-Ups**
- 12 **Ring Rows** *(or barbell rows / TRX rows)*
- 10 **DB or KB Push Press** (moderate-heavy)
- 250m **Concept2 Row**

**Goal Time:** **15–20 minutes**  
**Intent:** upper-body fatigue + lung burn

**Scaling**
- Push-ups: incline or reduce to **10**
- Rows: reduce to **8–10**
- Push press: lighter weight or **8 reps**
- Row: reduce to **200m**
