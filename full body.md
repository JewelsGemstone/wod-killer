# 🌿 Recovery-Focused Full Body Session (40–55 min)

## Warm-Up / Tissue Prep (10 min)
Move slowly and breathe through your nose.

- 4–5 min easy **Row or Echo Bike** (Zone 1–2)
- 2 rounds:
  - 8 × Cat–Cow
  - 10 × Glute Bridges
  - 10 × Band Pull-Aparts
  - 6 × World’s Greatest Stretch (alternating sides)

---

## Strength Through Range (Low Load)

### A. Controlled Circuit
3 rounds (no rushing):

- Goblet Squat – 8 reps (light, 3-second descent)
- Single-Arm Dumbbell Row – 10 reps per side (pause at top)
- Incline Push-Ups or DB Floor Press – 8–10 reps
- Farmer Carry – 40–60 sec (moderate load, posture focus)

Rest as needed. Prioritize full breathing before starting the next movement.

---

## Joint Health + Stability

### B. Lower Body / Core Flow
2–3 rounds:

- Reverse Lunges – 6 reps per side (slow and controlled)
- Single-Leg RDL (bodyweight or light KB) – 6 reps per side
- Dead Bug or Bird Dog – 6–8 reps per side
- Calf Raises – 12–15 reps (slow tempo)

---

## Light Conditioning (Parasympathetic Bias)
Choose **one**:

- 10–15 min easy walk (ideal if weather allows)  
**OR**
- 10 min easy Row or Bike (conversational pace)

---

## Cool Down (10–15 min)

- Couch Stretch – 1 min per side
- 90/90 Hip Stretch – 1 min per side
- Thoracic Rotations – 8 reps per side
- Child’s Pose with long exhales (4–6 sec)
- Optional: Legs up the wall – 3–5 min

---

## Recovery Intent
- Keep heart rate low
- Finish feeling looser and calmer
- No soreness tomorrow — just refreshed and primed
