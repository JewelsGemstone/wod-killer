# 🔥 25-Min EMOM Conditioning Workout

## Overview
High-output metabolic conditioning workout focused on endurance, work capacity, and mental toughness.

- **Format:** Every 5 minutes (E5MOM)
- **Rounds:** 5
- **Total Time:** 25 minutes

---

## 🏋️‍♂️ Workout

### Every 5 Minutes x 5 Rounds:
- 10 Burpees  
- 10 Kettlebell Squats  
- 10 EZ Bar Curls  
- 10 Burpees  

---

## 📊 Total Volume

| Movement            | Total Reps |
|--------------------|-----------|
| Burpees            | 100       |
| KB Squats          | 50        |
| EZ Bar Curls       | 50        |

---

## 🎯 Training Focus

- **Conditioning:** High heart rate, VO2 max, recovery between rounds  
- **Muscular Endurance:** Legs, arms, full-body fatigue  
- **Mental Toughness:** Sustained effort under fatigue  

---

## ⚠️ Gaps / Imbalance

This workout is effective but lacks:

- ❌ Pulling (rows, pull-ups)  
- ❌ Pushing strength (pressing)  
- ❌ Posterior chain (hinge movements)

---

## 🔧 Optimized Variations

### Option A – Balanced Conditioning

**Every 5 Minutes x 5 Rounds:**
- 10 Burpees  
- 10 KB Squats  
- 10 KB Swings or Deadlifts  
- 10 Pull-ups or Rows  

---

### Option B – Strength + Conditioning Hybrid

**Every 5 Minutes x 5 Rounds:**
- 8 Burpees  
- 8 Goblet Squats (heavier)  
- 8 Bent-Over Rows  
- 8 Push-ups or DB Press  

---

## 📅 Programming Notes

- Best used as a **conditioning day (1–2x per week)**  
- Avoid pairing with heavy lift days (deadlift, squat, bench)  
- Scale reps or weights to maintain intensity across all rounds  

---

## 🧠 Key Takeaway

> High-quality conditioning workout.  
> Add hinge + pulling movements to make it a complete, balanced session.
