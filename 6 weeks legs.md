# 6-Week Leg Strength Program
**Goal:** Increase lower-body strength (squat, hinge, unilateral stability)  
**Style:** One heavy lift per session + limited accessories  
**Frequency:** 3 days per week  
**Duration:** 6 weeks

---

## Weekly Structure

| Day | Focus |
|----|------|
| Day 1 | Heavy Squat (primary strength) |
| Day 2 | Posterior Chain (deadlift / hinge) |
| Day 3 | Unilateral + Volume (single-leg strength & stability) |

Rest at least **1 day** between sessions.

---

## Progression Philosophy

- Increase **load first**, reps second
- Leave **1–2 reps in reserve (RIR)** on all main lifts
- If bar speed slows drastically → hold weight steady next week
- Accessories stay challenging but controlled

---

## DAY 1 — Heavy Squat

### Main Lift
**Back Squat or Front Squat**

| Week | Prescription |
|----|-------------|
| 1 | 5 × 5 @ ~75% |
| 2 | 5 × 4 @ ~78% |
| 3 | 6 × 3 @ ~82% |
| 4 | 5 × 3 @ ~85% |
| 5 | 6 × 2 @ ~88% |
| 6 | 5 × 1 @ ~90–92% |

Rest **2–3 min**

---

### Accessories
- Romanian Deadlift — **3 × 8**
- Walking Lunges — **3 × 10 / leg**
- Standing Calf Raises — **3 × 15–20**

---

## DAY 2 — Posterior Chain Strength

### Main Lift (alternate weekly)
- **Odd weeks:** Conventional Deadlift  
- **Even weeks:** Trap Bar or RDL

| Week | Prescription |
|----|-------------|
| 1 | 4 × 5 |
| 2 | 5 × 4 |
| 3 | 5 × 3 |
| 4 | 6 × 3 |
| 5 | 5 × 2 |
| 6 | 4 × 2 (heavy but crisp) |

Load should feel **strong, not grindy**.

---

### Accessories
- Hip Thrusts or Glute Bridge — **3 × 8–10**
- Hamstring Curls (machine or band) — **3 × 12**
- Core: Planks or Dead Bugs — **3 rounds**

---

## DAY 3 — Unilateral + Volume

### Main Lift
**Split Squat or Bulgarian Split Squat**

| Week | Prescription |
|----|-------------|
| 1–2 | 3 × 8 / leg |
| 3–4 | 4 × 6 / leg |
| 5–6 | 5 × 5 / leg |

Load increases weekly.

---

### Secondary Lift
- Goblet Squat or Tempo Squat — **3 × 10**  
  *(3 sec down, 1 sec pause)*

---

### Accessories
- Step-Ups — **3 × 10 / leg**
- Single-Leg RDL — **3 × 8 / leg**
- Tibialis Raises or Band Work — **2–3 × 15**

---

## Optional Conditioning (1–2x / week)
*(Only if recovery is good)*

- Sled Pushes
- Bike intervals (short + hard)
- Hill walks or incline treadmill

Avoid conditioning **before heavy leg days**.

---

## Recovery Rules
- Eat **enough protein**
- Sleep > **7 hours**
- If joints feel beat up → reduce accessories, not main lifts

---

## Success Markers (End of Week 6)
- Squat and deadlift up **5–10%**
- Stronger unilateral balance
- No lingering knee or hip pain
- Better bar speed and confidence under load
