# Winter Daily Cardio Schedule (2-Mile Run Equivalent)

**Goal:** Maintain fitness and body composition through winter  
**Time Commitment:** 15–20 minutes per day  
**Equipment:** Concept2 Rower, Rogue Echo Bike  
**Baseline Equivalent:**  
- ~20 min steady cardio  
- OR ~15 min mixed/interval cardio  

---

## Weekly Split Overview

| Day | Machine | Focus |
|---|---|---|
| Monday | Rower | Steady aerobic |
| Tuesday | Echo Bike | Steady aerobic |
| Wednesday | Rower | Distance focus |
| Thursday | Echo Bike | Intervals |
| Friday | Rower | Relaxed aerobic |
| Saturday | Optional | Walk / Mobility |
| Sunday | Off | Recovery |

**Primary Split:**  
- **Rower:** 3 days/week  
- **Echo Bike:** 2 days/week  
- **Optional Active Recovery:** 1 day  
- **Full Rest:** 1 day  

---

## Daily Prescriptions

### Monday — Concept2 Rower (Steady)
- Distance: **4,000 meters**
- Stroke Rate: **20–24 spm**
- Effort: Conversational, controlled
- Time: ~16–18 minutes

🎯 *Closest 1:1 replacement for a 2-mile run*

---

### Tuesday — Echo Bike (Steady)
- Time: **15–20 minutes**
- Pace: Smooth, sustainable
- Breathing: Nasal or short-sentence capable

🔥 *High calorie burn with low joint stress*

---

### Wednesday — Concept2 Rower (Distance)
- Distance: **5,000 meters**
- Stroke Rate: **20–22 spm**
- Effort: Relaxed but continuous
- Time: ~20–22 minutes

🧠 *Mental endurance + aerobic base*

---

### Thursday — Echo Bike (Intervals)
- Format: **10 rounds**
  - 30 sec hard effort
  - 60 sec easy spin
- Total Time: ~15 minutes

⚡ *High metabolic effect in minimal time*

---

### Friday — Concept2 Rower (Easy)
- Time: **20 minutes**
- Stroke Rate: **18–22 spm**
- Focus: Smooth strokes, perfect form

🧘 *Recovery-friendly aerobic work*

---

### Saturday — Optional Active Recovery
- Dog walks
- Mobility / stretching
- Light kettlebells (optional)

🚶 *Movement without structured intensity*

---

### Sunday — Full Rest
- No structured cardio
- Gentle walking only

🛌 *Adaptation and recovery*

---

## Simple Rule for Busy Days

> **20 minutes on the rower**  
> **OR 15 minutes on the Echo Bike**  
= **2-mile run equivalent**

No substitutions required.

---

## Notes
- Keep rower damper between **3–5**
- Avoid “all-out” work more than once per week
- This schedule pairs well with **3–5 days of lifting**
- Designed to preserve muscle while controlling fat gain

---

## Progression (Optional)
- Add **500 m** to row days every 2–3 weeks
- OR add **1 interval** to Echo Bike sessions
- OR keep volume fixed and focus on smoother output

Consistency > intensity.
