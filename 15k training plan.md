# 🧠 Rules for Success

- Long run pace = slow conversational  
- Never increase long run more than ~0.5–1 mile  
- If joints ache → shorten distance, not frequency  
- Strength training stays 2× weekly  
- Avoid heavy leg day before long run  

---

## 🏃 Running Workout Definitions

### Strides
Short controlled accelerations lasting 15–25 seconds.

- Gradually speed up → hold fast relaxed pace → slow down
- NOT a sprint
- Focus on smooth form and quick light steps
- Walk 45–90 sec between strides

Purpose:
Improve running mechanics, leg turnover, and efficiency without fatigue.

---

### Tempo Run
Sustained "comfortably hard" running pace.

- Breathing deeper but controlled
- Can speak short phrases but not full sentences
- Effort about 6–7 out of 10

Purpose:
Improve endurance, aerobic efficiency, and ability to hold steady race pace.
