# 🔥 Kettlebell + Echo Bike / Concept2 Rower Hybrid Workouts

**Purpose:** Fun, high-energy conditioning that blends kettlebell strength with engine work  
**Use Cases:**  
- Finishers after heavy lifts  
- Standalone conditioning days  
- Hybrid strength + cardio sessions  

---

## 🚴‍♀️ WORKOUT 1 — ENGINE & IRON (Intervals)

**Format:** 5 Rounds  
**Rest:** 60 sec between rounds

### Echo Bike
- 20 calories (men) / 15 calories (women)

### Kettlebell Complex (Unbroken if possible)
- 5 KB Swings  
- 5 KB Cleans  
- 5 KB Front Squats  
- 5 KB Push Press  

**Notes:**  
- Switch arms each round  
- Focus on clean transitions under fatigue

---

## 🚣 WORKOUT 2 — ROW & FLOW (Aerobic + Skill)

**Format:** 4 Rounds (Quality over speed)  
**Rest:** 90 sec

### Concept2 Rower
- 500 meters

### Kettlebell Flow
- 6 KB Snatches (per side)  
- 6 KB Windmills  
- 8 Goblet Squats  

**Notes:**  
- Control breathing before starting the flow  
- Smooth, technical reps

---

## ⚡ WORKOUT 3 — DESCENT INTO MADNESS (Ladder)

**Format:** Descending Calories / Ascending KB Reps

### Echo Bike Calories
- 15 → 10 → 5

### Kettlebell Movements
- Goblet Squats  
- KB Swings  

### Rep Scheme
- 5 → 10 → 15

**Finisher**
- 100m Heavy Farmer’s Carry

**Notes:**  
- No rushing transitions  
- Grip will be challenged at the end

---

## 🧨 WORKOUT 4 — KB + ROWER CHIPPER

**Format:** For Time

1. 1,000m Row  
2. 50 KB Swings  
3. 750m Row  
4. 40 KB Clean & Press (20 per arm)  
5. 500m Row  
6. 30 KB Goblet Squats  
7. 250m Row  
8. 20 KB Snatches (10 per arm)

**Notes:**  
- Pace the first 1,000m  
- Push hard on the final 250m

---

## 🐍 WORKOUT 5 — THE PUNISHER (EMOM)

**Format:** 20-Minute EMOM  
**Cycle:** Repeat 5 times

| Minute | Work |
|------|------|
| 1 | Echo Bike – 15/12 calories |
| 2 | 12 KB Swings |
| 3 | 8 KB Clean + Press |
| 4 | Rest |

**Notes:**  
- Stay consistent — don’t sprint early  
- Missed reps carry into rest minute

---

## 🧠 WORKOUT 6 — SKILL UNDER FATIGUE

**Format:** 4 Rounds  
**Rest:** As needed between rounds

### Concept2 Rower
- 400 meters (moderate pace)

### Kettlebell
- Turkish Get-Up – 2 per side  
- KB Halos – 12 total  
- Suitcase Carry – 40 steps

**Notes:**  
- Focus on posture during carries  
- Keep TGUs slow and controlled

---

## 🏁 WORKOUT 7 — BIKE → BELL DEATH (Finisher)

**Format:** 3 Rounds (No Rest)

- Echo Bike – 30 sec all-out  
- KB Swings – 20  
- KB Push Press – 10  
- KB Front Rack Hold – 30 sec

**Notes:**  
- Use a challenging but sustainable KB  
- Front rack hold is the limiter

---

## ⚖️ PROGRAMMING GUIDANCE

| Scenario | Recommended Workouts |
|--------|---------------------|
| After heavy lifts | 1, 5, 7 |
| Conditioning day | 2, 4 |
| Low-impact but intense | 6 |
| Short on time | 3, 7 |

---

## 📓 LOGGING NOTES
- Track calories, meters, KB weight, and round times
- Increase intensity by:
  - Heavier KBs
  - Faster bike/row pace
  - Reduced rest
