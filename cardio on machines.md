# Cardio Session — Echo Bike + Concept2 Rower  
**Constraint:** Long blocks only (no rapid transitions)  
**Total Time:** ~35–40 minutes  
**Structure:** Rower first → Bike second (single transition)

---

## 1️⃣ Warm-Up (5–8 minutes)
- Easy **row** building from very light → moderate  
- Focus on smooth strokes and nasal breathing

---

## 2️⃣ Concept2 Rower — Aerobic + Threshold (20 minutes)

**5 × 4:00 WORK / 1:00 EASY**

- **Work Effort:** ~7/10  
  *(breathing hard but controlled)*
- **Stroke Rate:** 22–26 spm  
- **Cue:** Legs → hips → arms

**Goal:** Maintain consistent split across all work intervals

---

## 3️⃣ Transition (1–2 minutes)
- Hydrate
- Move from rower to Echo Bike
- Set monitor and resistance

---

## 4️⃣ Echo Bike — Power Endurance (15 minutes)

**10 × 1:00 ON / 0:30 EASY**

- **ON:** 8/10 effort  
  *(strong push, not a max sprint)*
- **EASY:** Very light spin

**Goal:** Hold calories steady across all intervals

---

## 5️⃣ Cooldown (5 minutes)
- Easy bike spin or walk
- Light stretching:
  - Quads
  - Hip flexors
  - Hamstrings

---

## Intensity Guidelines
- Finish **tired but functional**
- Recovery breathing returns within a few minutes
- Legs worked, not wrecked

---

## Scaling Options
- Reduce row work to **3:30**
- Bike intervals → **8 rounds**
- Keep effort capped at **6–7/10** for recovery days
