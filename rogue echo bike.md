# 🚴 Rogue Echo Bike Workout Library

A structured library of Rogue Echo Bike workouts focused on conditioning, endurance, fat loss, and mental toughness.

---

# 🧠 Programming Philosophy

Echo Bike training falls into 4 key categories:

- **HIIT / Sprint Intervals** → Power + fat loss  
- **Aerobic / Zone 2** → Endurance + recovery  
- **Mixed Modal** → Conditioning + strength  
- **Mental Grind** → Resilience + engine building  

---

# 🔥 1. HIIT / Sprint Workouts

## 1.1 Tabata
- 20 sec ALL OUT  
- 10 sec rest  
- x 8 rounds (4 min)

👉 Scale: 2–4 total sets

---

## 1.2 10/20 Intervals
- 10 sec sprint  
- 20 sec rest  
- x 10 rounds  

👉 2–3 sets (2 min rest between)

---

## 1.3 30/90 Intervals
- 30 sec hard (90–95%)  
- 90 sec easy spin  
- x 6–10 rounds  

---

## 1.4 Death By Calories
- Minute 1: 5 calories  
- Minute 2: 6 calories  
- Minute 3: 7 calories  
- Continue until failure  

---

# 🧱 2. Aerobic / Engine Work

## 2.1 Zone 2 Ride
- 20–40 minutes steady pace  
- Should be able to hold a conversation  

---

## 2.2 10-Min Max Calories
- Max calories in 10 minutes  
- Track progress monthly  

---

## 2.3 Interval Endurance
- 5 rounds:
  - 3 min moderate  
  - 1 min hard  

---

# ⚡ 3. Mixed Modal Workouts

## 3.1 21-15-9
- Echo Bike Calories  
- Burpees  
- Dumbbell Thrusters  

---

## 3.2 Conditioning Circuit
3–5 rounds:
- 15 cal bike  
- 10 kettlebell swings  
- 10 pushups  
- 10 goblet squats  

---

## 3.3 Buy-In / Cash-Out
- 50 cal bike  
- Workout  
- 50 cal bike  

---

## 3.4 EMOM Conditioning (20 min)
- Min 1: 12 cal bike  
- Min 2: 10 burpees  
- Min 3: Rest  

Repeat

---

# 🧠 4. Mental Toughness Workouts

## 4.1 5 x 1 Mile
- 1 mile hard  
- 30–60 sec rest  
- x 5  

---

## 4.2 100 Calorie Challenge
- 100 calories for time  

---

## 4.3 Pyramid Intervals
- 10 sec sprint / 50 sec rest  
- 20 / 40  
- 30 / 30  
- 40 / 20  
- 50 / 10  
- Then back down  

---

## 4.4 Every 5 Minutes x 5
Every 5 min:
- 20 cal bike  
- Rest remaining time
