# 🏋️ 6-Week Back Squat Strength Progression

**Goal:** Increase absolute strength while managing recovery  
**Frequency:** 1x per week  
**Style:** One heavy lift + complementary conditioning  
**Progression:** Intensity-driven with built-in deload

---

## Weekly Structure

- Warm-up + Back Squat (primary lift)
- Optional light accessory (non-fatiguing)
- Conditioning (Echo Bike + Kettlebell or similar)

---

## Week 1 – Base Volume

- Back Squat  
  - 4 × 5 @ 75%  
  - Rest: 2–3 min  
- **Focus:** Technique, depth, bracing

**Optional**
- Tempo Goblet Squat (3-1-1) — 2 × 8

---

## Week 2 – Volume + Load

- Back Squat  
  - 5 × 5 @ 77–78%  
  - Rest: 2.5–3 min  
- **Focus:** Consistent bar path, smooth ascent

---

## Week 3 – Strength Transition

- Back Squat  
  - 5 × 4 @ 80–82%  
  - Rest: 3 min  
- **Focus:** Controlled descent, aggressive drive

**Optional**
- Pause Squat (1-sec) — 2 × 3 @ ~70%

---

## Week 4 – Heavy Triples

- Back Squat  
  - 5 × 3 @ 85–87%  
  - Rest: 3–4 min  
- **Focus:** Max tension, perfect reps

---

## Week 5 – Peak Strength

- Back Squat  
  - 6 × 2 @ 88–92%  
  - Rest: 3–4 min  
- **Focus:** Speed off the bottom, strong bracing

> All reps should feel powerful. No grinding.

---

## Week 6 – Deload & Reset

- Back Squat  
  - 3 × 5 @ 65–70%  
  - Rest: 2 min  
- **Focus:** Recovery, bar speed, mobility

**Optional**
- Goblet Squat — 2 × 10 (easy)

---

## Progression Notes

- Increase load week-to-week only if all reps are clean  
- If a week feels heavy, repeat the load rather than forcing progression  
- Maintain at least **48–72 hours** before next heavy lower-body work  

---

## Post-Cycle Options

- Retest 3RM or 1RM in Week 7  
- Roll directly into another 6-week cycle at +2.5–5%  
- Transition to front squats or tempo squats for variety
