# 🔥 Burpee Ladder Conditioning Workout

A simple, brutal, and effective full-body conditioning workout using a **descending burpee ladder**.  
No equipment required.

---

## 🧠 Warm-Up (5 Minutes)

Complete **2 rounds**:

- 30 sec Jumping Jacks  
- 30 sec Air Squats  
- 30 sec Plank Hold  
- 30 sec Walk-Outs  
- 60 sec Easy Burpees  
  - Step-back  
  - No jump  

---

## 💥 Main Workout — Descending Burpee Ladder

### Structure
- Start at **10 burpees**
- Work down to **1 burpee**
- After **each burpee set**, perform:
  - **10 Air Squats**
  - **10 Sit-Ups** (or Dead Bugs)

### Ladder Example

| Round | Burpees | Air Squats | Sit-Ups |
|------:|--------:|-----------:|--------:|
| 1 | 10 | 10 | 10 |
| 2 | 9 | 10 | 10 |
| 3 | 8 | 10 | 10 |
| 4 | 7 | 10 | 10 |
| 5 | 6 | 10 | 10 |
| 6 | 5 | 10 | 10 |
| 7 | 4 | 10 | 10 |
| 8 | 3 | 10 | 10 |
| 9 | 2 | 10 | 10 |
| 10 | 1 | 10 | 10 |

---

## ⏱ Time Targets

- **Beginner:** Finish the ladder  
- **Intermediate:** < 25 minutes  
- **Advanced:** < 18–20 minutes  

---

## 🧱 Optional Finisher

Complete **3 rounds**:

- 30 sec Fast Burpees  
- 30 sec Plank Hold  
- 30 sec Rest  

---

## 🔧 Scaling Options

### Easier
- Step back instead of jumping
- Remove the push-up
- Start ladder at **6** instead of 10

### Harder
- Add push-up + tuck jump to burpees
- Wear a weighted vest
- Replace air squats with **jump squats**
- Run the ladder **EMOM-style**

---

## 🧘 Cooldown (5 Minutes)

- Child’s Pose — 1 min  
- Hip Flexor Stretch — 1 min/side  
- Chest & Shoulder Stretch — 1 min  
- Deep Breathing — 1 min  

---

## ✅ Notes

- Move with intention — **clean reps > speed**
- Scale early to avoid burnout
- Excellent standalone workout or finisher
