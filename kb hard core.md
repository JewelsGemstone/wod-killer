# ☠️ Kettlebell + Echo Bike / Concept2 Rower — HARD MODE

**Audience:** Advanced athletes  
**Expectation:** Heavy kettlebells, aggressive pacing, mental toughness  
**Rule:** If you finish feeling “fine,” you didn’t go hard enough

---

## 🚴‍♀️ WORKOUT 1 — ENGINE & IRON: HEAVY EDITION

**Format:** 6 Rounds  
**Rest:** 45 sec (max)

### Echo Bike
- 25 calories (men) / 20 calories (women)

### Kettlebell Complex (Unbroken)
- 6 KB Swings  
- 6 KB Cleans  
- 6 KB Front Squats  
- 6 KB Push Press  

**Rules**
- Same bell for entire complex  
- If you drop the bell → 10 penalty swings

---

## 🚣 WORKOUT 2 — ROW & FLOW: GRIND VERSION

**Format:** 5 Rounds  
**Rest:** 60 sec

### Concept2
- 750 meters (hold uncomfortable pace)

### Kettlebell Flow (No Setting Bell Down)
- 8 KB Snatches (per side)  
- 8 KB Windmills  
- 12 Goblet Squats (3-sec tempo down)

**Rules**
- One bell, one grip change allowed  
- Missed tempo = repeat goblet squats

---

## ⚡ WORKOUT 3 — DESCENT INTO DARKNESS (EXTENDED LADDER)

**Format:** Descending Bike / Ascending KB

### Echo Bike Calories
- 30 → 25 → 20 → 15 → 10

### Kettlebell Movements
- Goblet Squats  
- KB Swings  

### Rep Scheme
- 10 → 15 → 20 → 25 → 30

**Finisher**
- 200m Heavy Farmer’s Carry  
- Bell must not touch the ground

---

## 🧨 WORKOUT 4 — ROW + KB CHIPPER: ELITE

**Format:** For Time (Time Cap: 40 min)

1. 2,000m Row  
2. 100 KB Swings  
3. 1,500m Row  
4. 80 KB Clean & Press (40/arm)  
5. 1,000m Row  
6. 60 KB Front Squats  
7. 500m Row  
8. 40 KB Snatches (20/arm)

**Rules**
- Swings must reach chest height  
- No more than 2 breaks per movement

---

## 🐍 WORKOUT 5 — THE PUNISHER: NO-REST EMOM

**Format:** 30-Minute EMOM  
**Cycle:** Repeat 6 times

| Minute | Work |
|------|------|
| 1 | Echo Bike – 20/16 calories |
| 2 | 15 Heavy KB Swings |
| 3 | 10 KB Clean + Push Press |
| 4 | 12 Goblet Squats |
| 5 | Rest |

**Rules**
- Fail a minute → burpees until next minute starts

---

## 🧠 WORKOUT 6 — SKILL UNDER SUFFOCATION

**Format:** 5 Rounds  
**Rest:** As needed (but regret it)

### Concept2
- 500m Row (above threshold)

### Kettlebell Control
- Turkish Get-Up – 3 per side  
- KB Halos – 20 total  
- Front Rack Carry – 50 steps (switch halfway)

**Rules**
- No jogging the carry  
- Missed lockout = redo rep

---

## 🏁 WORKOUT 7 — BIKE → BELL → SOUL LEAVE BODY

**Format:** 4 Rounds  
**Rest:** None

- Echo Bike – 40 sec ALL-OUT  
- KB Swings – 30  
- KB Push Press – 20  
- KB Front Rack Hold – 45 sec  
- Plank Hold – 60 sec

**Rules**
- Front rack must stay high  
- Drop the plank → restart the minute

---

## ⚖️ OPTIONAL SUFFERING MODIFIERS

- Wear a weight vest  
- Use competition bells only  
- Nose breathing only on rows  
- No chalk  
- Add 5 burpees between rounds

---

## 📓 PERFORMANCE METRICS
Track:
- Calories/min (Bike)
- Split consistency (Row)
- KB weight used
- Number of drops
- Time to emotional collapse

- Destroy grip

- Build elite conditioning

Use 2–3 per week max unless you hate yourself.
